================================================================================
                        B A L L O T I F Y
                  How the app works: an illustrated guide
================================================================================

Ballotify lets an ORGANISER run an online vote (election, awards, poll) and lets
VOTERS take part from any phone or laptop using a one-time code.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE PEOPLE INVOLVED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   ORGANISER   Creates an account, builds the ballot, issues codes, shares the
               link, watches turnout, announces results.
               -> needs to sign in.

   CO-ORGANISER  Invited by the owner (Pro): Editor, Voter manager or Viewer.
               -> signs in with the invited email; sees only what the role allows.

   VOTER       Receives a link + a personal code (or requests their own with
               an ID on the list, if allowed). Votes once, online or by USSD.
               -> no account, no app to install.

   FAN         Paid voting: picks a nominee and pays per vote. Can vote again.

   NOMINATOR   Suggests nominees before voting (if nominations are on).

   CANDIDATE   Optional private link to add their own photo and description.

   AUDIENCE    Anyone with the results link (e.g. a projector at the venue).
               -> sees totals only, never who voted for whom.
               Anyone can check a results certificate at /verify.

3. LIFECYCLE OF AN EVENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   The status is worked out automatically from the voting window you set:

        starts_at                                   ends_at
            |                                          |
   ---------+==========================================+--------------> time
   SCHEDULED|                 LIVE                     |    CLOSED
   (upcoming)            (ballot accepts votes)        |  (completed)
   ballot hidden,                                      |  ballot refused,
   shows countdown                                     |  results remain

   - No start time  -> live immediately.
   - No end time    -> stays live until you close it.
   - "Close voting now" button (Settings) ends it instantly.

4. THE ORGANISER'S JOURNEY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   Plans: every event starts FREE. Drafts are unlimited; 1 LIVE event at a
   time (an event goes live when it gets its first voters), 30 voters,
   codes shared on WhatsApp / CSV / printed slips, "Powered by Ballotify".
   Upgrading ONE event to PRO is a one-off Paystack payment: 5,000 voters,
   email & SMS invitations and reminders, own logo and colour, up to 10
   co-organisers. (Prices/limits: Admin -> Pricing.)

   Two ways to vote, chosen per event:
     SECRET BALLOT (codes)  one-time code per voter, one vote each, receipt
                            after voting to check it was counted.
     PAID PUBLIC VOTING     award nights: anyone picks a nominee and pays per
                            vote on Paystack; the platform keeps a fee and
                            pays the rest to the organiser.

   [ Sign up ] --> [ Create event ] --> ( Nominations ) --> [ Build ballot ]
                                          optional               |
                                                                 v
   [ Certify & announce ] <-- [ Watch results ] <-- [ Share ] <-- [ Issue codes ]

   Step by step:

   (1) CREATE EVENT
       Pick a template (Church, AGM, Board, Award night, School) or a blank
       event, then: name, description, how people vote (secret ballot or
       paid voting + price), who sees results, opening/closing time.
       The voting method is set per category on the Ballot tab:
         "Choose one" (default)   most votes wins
         "Pick up to N"           voters pick several; most votes wins
         "Approval"               voters tick everyone they approve of;
                                  results show % of voters (can add up
                                  to more than 100%)
         "Ranked choice"          voters rank candidates 1st, 2nd, 3rd...
                                  Counted in rounds (instant runoff):

           Round 1: count everyone's 1st choice.
           Anyone over half of the votes still counting?  -> they win.
           Otherwise the last-placed candidate is out, and each of their
           ballots moves to that voter's next choice still in the race.
           Repeat. (Ballots with no choices left stop counting.)
           A tie for last is broken by the earlier rounds; if everyone
           left is tied, they share the win.

         "Motion" (Add motion)    a proposal voters answer Yes / No /
                                  Abstain. Passes by simple majority (more
                                  Yes than No), two-thirds or three-quarters
                                  of the Yes + No votes; abstentions don't
                                  count towards the majority.

       WHO CAN VOTE (per category, Ballot tab): "Everyone" or only some voter
       groups. Voters get groups from a Group / Branch / Unit column when
       importing, or the Groups field. A voter only sees, and can only vote
       in, the categories for their groups (checked by the server, on the web
       and USSD). Results show "x of y eligible voters" for those categories.

       CAMPAIGN CARDS: the image icon next to a candidate (Ballot tab), the
       candidate's own profile page, and a share button on paid-voting
       nominees make a picture with the photo, name, category and a QR code
       to the voting page. Square for posts, tall for WhatsApp status.

         Results show "How the count went" round by round; the report,
         CSV, live reveal and certificates use the same count. On USSD,
         voters rank by picking their 1st choice, then 2nd... (0 = done).

       Already ran this vote last year? Settings -> Advanced -> "Duplicate
       event" copies the whole ballot (no voters, votes or dates).

       SETTINGS TAB is split into four sections:
         General ........ name, dates, voting mode, results visibility
         Voting options . self-registration, USSD, nominations (switches)
         Branding ....... your logo and colour (Pro)
         Advanced ....... duplicate, close voting, erase contacts, delete

   (1b) NOMINATIONS (optional, before voting)
       Voting options -> Nominations ON, set when they close (before voting
       opens), share the link /nominate/<link>. The public suggests nominees
       per category (max 3 per person per category; bot check). The
       Nominations tab groups them by name with counts:

         President                                   2 nominees
          1  Kemi Adeyemi   "Always shows up..."  14   [+ Add to ballot]
          2  Tunde Bello                           6   [+ Add to ballot]

       Nominations close automatically at the first vote.

   (2) BUILD THE BALLOT  (Ballot tab)

         Event: Annual Elections 2026  
           |
           +-- Category: President
           |      +-- Candidate: Ada Obi      [photo]  "Member since 2015"     
           |      +-- Candidate: Bola Ade     [photo]   (description is optional)
           |
           +-- Category: Treasurer
                  +-- Candidate: Chidi Eze
                  +-- Candidate: Dayo Musa

         Many candidates? "Import candidates" takes a spreadsheet with
         Category, Name, Description columns and creates missing categories.

         CANDIDATE PROFILES (optional): the link icon next to a candidate
         gives a private link (copy or send on WhatsApp). The candidate opens
         it, adds their own photo and short description; they can't change
         their name or category. Every change is in the Activity log. The
         organiser can switch the link off at any time.

   (3) ISSUE VOTER CODES  (Voters tab)

         Option A: "Generate 500"  -> 500 anonymous codes
         Option B: "Import"        -> paste/upload a list (name, email, phone,
                                    optional ID such as membership no.),
                                    1 code per person
         Option C: "Add"           -> one voter: name, email, phone, ID, code

         Codes look like:  K7M2QX9P   (8 chars, no 0/O or 1/I/L look-alikes)
         With USSD voting on:  4831907265  (10 digits: keypads can't type
         letters; "Switch existing codes to numbers" converts old ones)

         VOTER SELF-REGISTRATION (optional, Pro). You can still send codes
         yourself. If switched on, the ballot shows "Don't have your code?":

           voter types their ID  -->  found on YOUR list?  --no-->  "not on the list"
                                            | yes
                                            v
                 code sent ONLY to the email/phone YOU have on file
                 ("Sent to ad••@gmail.com"), max once every 2 minutes

         Knowing someone's membership number is not enough to vote as them.

         Then:  [Send codes]  -> by Email / SMS / Both (link + personal code)
                [Download CSV]  or  [Print slips]

         Each voter row shows:  Not invited -> Invited -> Voted
         (or "Not delivered" with the reason, e.g. wrong number).
         Codes are hidden as ******** in the list; click the eye to reveal.

         +---------------------------------------------------+
         |  [QR]   Annual Elections 2026                     |
         |  [QR]   Ada Obi                                   |
         |  [QR]   YOUR VOTING CODE:  K7M2QX9P               |
         |         https://yoursite.com/vote/3f2c...          |
         +------------------ cut here -----------------------+

   (4) SHARE  (Share tab)
         - Voting link .......... send to voters
         - Live results link .... show on a screen / share later
         - QR code .............. posters and slides
         - Message template ..... ready for email / WhatsApp / SMS

   (5) MONITOR
         Overview tab: turnout bar, setup checklist.
         Voters tab:   who has voted (Voted / Not voted), never for whom.
                       A used code can't be reset: votes are secret, so a
                       cast vote can't be found or undone.
         Results tab:  the organiser's view, whatever the public setting.
         Activity tab: every change made to the event, in order.

   (6) ANNOUNCE
         Results tab -> [CSV] spreadsheet of the full tally
                     -> [Report / PDF] the full record of the vote, saved as
                        a PDF from the browser's print window (A4):
                          page 1: headline numbers (ballots cast, registered
                                  voters, turnout, categories, candidates,
                                  votes), the winners with share and margin
                                  (ties marked), the turnout chart, and the
                                  certificate number + QR code if certified.
                          then:   every category, every candidate: votes,
                                  % share bars, winner highlighted; the
                                  integrity statement; optional signature
                                  lines (returning officer, witness).
                          appendix (optional, on by default once anyone has
                                  voted): the voter register: each voter, their
                                  member ID, "Voted"/"Did not vote" and the
                                  time. Never HOW anyone voted: that isn't
                                  stored.
                        Paid events show votes, payments and a payments
                        summary appendix (raised, platform fee, organiser's
                        share, the latest 50 payments) instead.
                        "Changes after voting began" counts only edits to
                        candidates/categories made once votes existed.
                     -> [Present] / [Reveal] full screen on a projector.

         CERTIFY (after voting closes): Results tab -> "Certify results".
         Ballotify signs a snapshot of the final totals and gives it a number
         (BLT-XXXX-XXXX). The printable certificate has a QR code. Anyone can
         open /verify/<number> and see one of:
           [OK]    Genuine; matches the event's current totals
           [WARN]  Genuine, but the totals have changed since it was issued
           [BAD]   Not valid (the stored certificate was altered)

         PAID VOTING MONEY: Earnings tab. Pick your bank and account number,
         Ballotify checks the account name with Paystack, then turns on
         AUTOMATIC PAYOUTS: Paystack splits every vote payment, sending your
         share straight to your bank and the platform fee to Ballotify.
         Without it, the admin pays you by transfer (Admin -> Payouts).

   Once the FIRST vote is cast the ballot is LOCKED: no adding, removing
   or moving categories/candidates. Name and photo fixes are still allowed
   and are logged; the results page shows how many happened.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. THE VOTER'S JOURNEY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   LANGUAGE: every voting, results and nominations page has a language
   button (English, Yorùbá, Hausa, Igbo, Français). Pages open in ENGLISH
   by default. They open in another language only if:
     the voter picked one earlier (remembered on their phone), or
     the organiser set one for the event (Settings -> Voting options).
   Messages from the server ("code already used", "voting has ended"...)
   come back in the same language.

   IDLE TIMEOUT: once a code is typed in, a ballot (or the thank-you screen,
   or a paid-vote payment form) left untouched for 1 minute shows "Are you
   still there?" with a 30-second countdown, then closes: the code is
   cleared and any choices are thrown away. Nothing is submitted, so the
   voter can enter the same code again. Protects shared phones and
   polling-booth tablets.

   Opens link            Enters code            Picks candidates
   +------------+        +------------+         +----------------+
   | Annual     |        | Enter your |         | PRESIDENT      |
   | Elections  |  --->  | code       |  --->   | (o) Ada Obi    |
   | 2026       |        | [K7M2QX9P] |         | ( ) Bola Ade   |
   | LIVE       |        | [Continue] |         | TREASURER      |
   | closes in  |        +------------+         | ( ) Chidi Eze  |
   | 3h 12m     |              |                | (o) Dayo Musa  |
   +------------+              |                | [Review]       |
                               |                +----------------+
                     wrong / used code?                 |
                     -> friendly error,                 v
                        try again               +----------------+
                                                | Review ballot  |
                                                | President: Ada |
        +---------------------------+           | Treasurer: Dayo|
        |   (check) Your vote has   |  <------  | [Change][Cast] |
        |   been recorded           |           +----------------+
        |   [View live results]     |
        +---------------------------+

   - Blank categories are allowed (a voter may abstain).
   - If the event isn't open yet: a countdown is shown instead of the ballot
     (plus "Nominate someone" while nominations are open).
   - If it has closed: "Voting has closed" + a button to see results.
   - After voting: a RECEIPT (e.g. K7QM-3XPA-9D). On the results page,
     "Check your ballot" confirms it was counted, without showing the choice.

   VOTING BY USSD (no smartphone or data needed; Pro, Africa's Talking):

     Dial *384*1234#
       CON Welcome to Ballotify          -> types event number 476235
       CON Annual Elections 2026
           Enter your voting code:       -> types 4831907265
       CON President (1/4)
           1. Ada Obi  2. Bola Ade
           0. Skip                       -> 1   (one screen per category;
                                                 "98. More" for long lists)
       CON Confirm your vote:
           President: Ada Obi ...
           1. Submit  2. Cancel          -> 1
       END Thank you! Your vote was recorded.
           Receipt: R5H8-V37P-K2

     Same rules as the web ballot (same code, once only, secret). On USSD a
     voter picks one candidate per category. The phone number isn't stored.

6. WHAT HAPPENS WHEN A VOTE IS SUBMITTED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   Voter presses "Cast my vote"
            |
            v
   +---------------------------------+     no
   | Is the event inside its voting  |-----------> 403 "Voting has closed /
   | window right now?               |              hasn't started"
   +---------------------------------+
            | yes
            v
   +---------------------------------+     no
   | Does every chosen candidate     |-----------> 422 "Invalid candidate"
   | belong to this event AND to the |
   | category it was chosen under?   |
   +---------------------------------+
            | yes
            v
   +---------------------------------+     no
   | Single-choice event: at most    |-----------> 422 "Only one candidate
   | one pick per category?          |              per category"
   +---------------------------------+
            | yes
            v
   ==================== DATABASE TRANSACTION BEGINS =====================
   +---------------------------------+
   | LOCK this voter's code row      |   <- a second, simultaneous submit
   +---------------------------------+      with the same code has to WAIT
            |                               here, then sees "already used"
            v
   +---------------------------------+     no
   | Code exists and is unused?      |-----------> 422 "Invalid or used code"
   +---------------------------------+
            | yes
            v
   +---------------------------------+
   | Add 1 to each chosen candidate's|   <- SECRET BALLOT: only totals
   |   running total                 |      change. No record of WHO chose
   | Mark code as used               |      WHAT is ever written.
   +---------------------------------+
   ==================== TRANSACTION COMMITS =============================
            |
            v
   Broadcast "the totals changed" (no ballot content) ---> live results
   pages refresh (only when results are public)
            |
            v
   200 "Vote recorded" ---> voter sees the confirmation screen

   Also: code checks and vote submissions are limited to 30 per minute
   per network per event, so codes cannot be guessed by brute force.

7. LIVE RESULTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   +---------------------------------------------------------------+
   |  * LIVE RESULTS   Annual Elections 2026     [Refresh][Present]|
   |  Voting closes in 3h 12m                                      |
   +---------------------------------------------------------------+
   | Votes cast 1,284 | Turnout 62% | Categories 2 | Candidates 4  |
   +---------------------------------------------------------------+
   | President                          Leading: Ada Obi           |
   |    .-"""-.     1  Ada Obi    ################......  58.1%    |
   |   /  642  \    2  Bola Ade   ##############........  41.9%    |
   |   \ votes /                                                   |
   |    '-...-'                                                    |
   +---------------------------------------------------------------+

   Who can see it (chosen per event in Settings):
     - Live ............... anyone with the link, while voting runs
     - After voting closes  a countdown until the end, then the totals
                            (this is the default)
     - Only me ............ never published; the organiser sees it in the
                            dashboard's Results tab and announces it

   Trust line on the page:
     "Secret ballot · no changes were made to this ballot after voting
      began"  (or how many recorded changes there were)

   How it stays up to date:
     - With Laravel Reverb running: every accepted ballot sends a signal
       and the page refreshes within about a second.
     - Without it: the page refreshes itself every 15 seconds.
     - "Present" switches to full screen for projectors.
     - After closing, the badge changes to "Final results" and the leader
       is labelled "Winner". Ties are shown as "Tied: A & B".

8. HOW THE DATA FITS TOGETHER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   users (organisers)
     |
     | 1 organiser has many events
     v
   events
     |  name, description, link_token (the secret part of the voting URL),
     |  starts_at, ends_at, voting_mode (codes / paid), vote_price_naira,
     |  brand colour + logo, scheduled reminder, plan (free / pro),
     |  self_registration + ID label, ussd_enabled + event number,
     |  nominations_enabled + nominations_ends_at
     |
     +--< categories           e.g. "President"; method (plurality | ranked),
     |       |                 max_choices (1 = choose one, N = up to N,
     |       |                 empty = any / approval), shuffle flag,
     |       |                 ballots (how many voters answered it)
     |       |
     |       +--< ranked_ballots  ranked choice only: one ranking per ballot,
     |       |                 with NO voter id, NO time, random id
     |       |
     |       +--< candidates   e.g. "Ada Obi" (+ photo, + votes = running total,
     |                         + optional private profile-link token)
     |
     +--< voters               one row per code; used_at = THAT it voted
     |                         (never for whom); optional member ID
     |
     +--< ballot_receipts      hashed receipt codes; NOT linked to a voter
     |                         or a choice
     |
     +--< nominations          public nominations (name, reason; IP kept
     |                         only as a hash, to limit repeats)
     +--< result_certificates  signed snapshot of the final results
     +--< vote_purchases       paid votes (Paystack reference, quantity, fee,
     |                         subaccount when split to the organiser)
     +--< payouts              money sent to the organiser for paid voting
     +--< event_members        co-organisers: editor / voters / viewer
     |
     +--< audit_logs           every organiser change, with "during voting"
                               flag; append-only

   - There is no table of individual ballots: that's what makes the
     ballot secret. Results = each candidate's running total.
   - Deleting an event deletes everything under it.
   - Voter emails / phones are erased automatically 30 days after voting
     closes (configurable), or at any time from Settings.

9. SECURITY & INTEGRITY SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   [x] One vote per code, guaranteed under heavy concurrency (row lock).
   [x] Voting window enforced by the server, not just hidden in the page.
   [x] Choices validated against the event, the category and the category's
       pick limit.
   [x] Ranked choice keeps each ranking (to count the rounds) but nothing
       that links it to a voter: no voter id, no timestamp, random ids.
   [x] Voters get a receipt to check their ballot was counted, without it
       revealing how they voted.
   [x] Paid votes count once, only for the full amount, and only if paid
       before voting closed.
   [x] Brute-force protection on code entry and vote submission.
   [x] Bot / card-testing check (Cloudflare Turnstile) on sign-up, paid-vote
       checkout, nominations and self-registration, once its keys are set.
   [x] Self-registration sends codes only to contact details the organiser
       has on file, never to an address the voter types in.
   [x] USSD voting goes through the same checks as the web ballot; its
       callback URL holds a secret, and the phone number isn't stored.
   [x] Results certificates are signed (HMAC with the app key): an edited
       certificate fails verification, and changed totals are flagged.
   [x] Payout bank accounts are verified with Paystack before payouts start.
   [x] Organisers can only see and change their own events (others get 404);
       co-organisers get only what their role allows.
   [x] Nested items can't be edited through another event's URL.
   [x] Secret ballot: only totals are stored; nobody can see how anyone voted.
   [x] Ballot locks at the first vote; later fixes are logged and disclosed.
   [x] Results published live, after close, or never, as the organiser chooses.
   [x] Passwords hashed; changing a password requires the current one and
       signs out other devices. Password reset by email.
   [x] Optional two-step login with Google Authenticator (+ recovery codes).
       Each code works once; 5 wrong codes lock the second step for 15 min.
   [x] Payments: Paystack first, Flutterwave as automatic backup. Whichever
       took the payment, it is re-checked with that provider before votes
       or upgrades are applied, and only once.
   [x] Logins expire (7 days by default).
   [x] Email must be confirmed before codes can be sent to voters.
   [x] Voter contact details erased automatically after the event.
   [x] Organisers can download their data and delete their account
       (Account page), in line with NDPA / GDPR rights.
   [x] Uploaded photos are type-checked and size-limited (5 MB).
   [x] CSV exports are protected against spreadsheet formula injection.
   [x] The platform owner can suspend an abusive organiser: they are signed
       out, can't sign in, and their voting/results links stop working.
       The admin area never shows ballots, voter lists or codes.
